\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This research develops and evaluates neural-network models based on long short-term memory (LSTM) and transformers adapted to time-series regression tasks. Different configurations and architectures are studied to compare their performance in single-output and multi-output forecasting problems. The experimental process includes data preprocessing, model training, and evaluation using metrics such as RMSE, MAE, MAPE, and $R^2$.

\vspace{0.5cm}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} time series, LSTM, transformers, neural networks, regression.
